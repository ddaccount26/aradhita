About Me

I'm a technology enthusiast with a passion for building AI solutions and educating the next generation of tech professionals. My journey has taken me from the halls of NIT Calicut and IIT Delhi to the corporate world at HSBC and Phreesia, and now to entrepreneurship with Career Nexus Academy.

At my core, I believe in the transformative power of education. My mission is to democratize AI and tech education across India, making high-quality learning accessible to students from all backgrounds. Through Career Nexus Academy, I'm building an ecosystem that bridges the gap between education and employment in the tech industry.

Mission Statement

"To empower 1 million Indian students with practical AI and tech skills by 2030, creating a new generation of problem-solvers and innovators."
